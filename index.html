Mbabane

Mbabane is the capital city of Eswatini, located in the Hhohho Region in the northwestern part of the country. It is the country's administrative and political centre, housing government offices, businesses, and important institutions. The city is surrounded by beautiful hills and mountains, giving it a scenic landscape. Mbabane has a relatively cool climate, a growing urban population, and serves as an important centre for education, healthcare, commerce, and tourism.
