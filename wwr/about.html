History

Born in the shadow of the great granite dome, Sibebe Water Rafting started with one raft, a few friends and a love of the river.

Today we guide thousands of paddlers each year through gorges, rapids and golden sunsets. Our mission is to provide unforgettable adventures while sharing the beauty of nature.
